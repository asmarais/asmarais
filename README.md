### 🚀 Featured Projects

**[Claimora AI](https://github.com/asmarais/automate-or-die)** — Trilingual (French/Arabic/derja) first-notice-of-loss assistant for Tunisian auto insurance claims. Guarded RAG architecture where the LLM phrases responses but a deterministic backend owns all facts — Groq Whisper transcription, vision-based damage analysis, explainable risk scoring.

**[RailMind](https://github.com/ouemnaa/rail-mind)** — AI railway conflict detection & resolution platform (team project) — hybrid mathematical optimization + LLM resolution agents, XGBoost prediction (87% AUROC), Qdrant semantic search over 113 historical incidents. My contribution: frontend and system integration.

**[Agentify](https://github.com/asmarais/Agentify)** — Agentify is an AI-powered insurance assistant that combines recommendation models, **LangGraph agentic workflows**, and **Llama 3.2 via Ollama** to analyze client data, recommend relevant insurance products, and generate personalized commercial pitches. It also supports conversational pitch refinement, client analytics, and multi-channel communication through a **FastAPI** backend.

**[PFE Management Platform](https://github.com/bahaell/PFE_Management_Platform)** — Microservices platform (team project) for managing graduation projects — Spring Boot, Eureka service discovery, distributed tracing via Zipkin.
